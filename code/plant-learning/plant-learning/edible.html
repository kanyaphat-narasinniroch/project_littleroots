Cooking and Plant Consumption

Cooking basics for garden plants

Wash plants well, cook shoots and roots when a recipe says so, and start with small portions of new foods.

Precautions before eating

Only eat plants you can identify for sure. Some plants look alike, and some are not safe raw. Ask a teacher, a parent or a local expert if you are unsure.
