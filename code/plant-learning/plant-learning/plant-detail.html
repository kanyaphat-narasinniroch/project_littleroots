กะเพรา – Holy basil

Template page. To add a new plant, copy this file into plants/, change the text and the image names, then link to it from a category page. Every plant has its own page in the plants/ folder.

Herbal Plants
Thai name
กะเพรา
Scientific name
Ocimum tenuiflorum
Plant family
Lamiaceae
Botanical features
A small shrub about 30–60 cm tall with fragrant, slightly hairy leaves and spikes of small pink-purple flowers.
Origin and habitat
Grown across tropical Asia; very common in Thai home gardens and pots.

Uses and benefits

The leaves are a key ingredient in pad kaprao and other quick stir-fries. Households also grow it as a fragrant herb.

Precautions

Traditional uses are widely reported, but this site makes no medical claims. If you are pregnant, take medicine, or have a health condition, ask a doctor before using herbs in large amounts.
