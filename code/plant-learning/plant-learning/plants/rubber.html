ยางพารา – Para rubber tree

Economic Crops
Thai name
ยางพารา
Scientific name
Hevea brasiliensis
Plant family
Euphorbiaceae
Botanical features
A tall tree with smooth bark and leaves made of three leaflets. Cutting the bark releases white latex.
Origin and habitat
Native to the Amazon basin; grown in plantations in southern and eastern Thailand.

Uses and benefits

Latex is collected by tapping, then made into sheets, gloves, tyres and other rubber goods.

Precautions

Natural rubber latex can cause allergies in some people.
