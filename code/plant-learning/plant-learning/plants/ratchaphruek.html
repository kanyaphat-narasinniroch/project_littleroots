ราชพฤกษ์ – Golden shower tree

Ornamental Plants
Thai name
ราชพฤกษ์
Scientific name
Cassia fistula
Plant family
Fabaceae
Botanical features
A medium tree with long hanging clusters of bright yellow flowers and long, dark pods.
Origin and habitat
Native to South and Southeast Asia; planted along roads and in temples and gardens.

Uses and benefits

Valued as a shade tree, and its golden flowers are linked with royalty and good fortune in Thai culture.

Precautions

The tree is mainly decorative. Do not eat pods or seeds without expert guidance.
