มันสำปะหลัง – Cassava

Economic Crops
Thai name
มันสำปะหลัง
Scientific name
Manihot esculenta
Plant family
Euphorbiaceae
Botanical features
A woody shrub with hand-shaped leaves and thick, starchy roots that grow underground.
Origin and habitat
Originally from South America; widely grown on Thai farmland.

Uses and benefits

Roots are cut and dried into chips for animal feed or ground into starch used in food and industry.

Precautions

Raw cassava can contain natural toxins and must be properly processed or cooked before eating.
