ขมิ้นชัน – Turmeric

Herbal Plants
Thai name
ขมิ้นชัน
Scientific name
Curcuma longa
Plant family
Zingiberaceae
Botanical features
A clumping plant with large green leaves. The underground stem (rhizome) is bright orange inside.
Origin and habitat
Native to South Asia; grown throughout Southeast Asia in warm, moist soil.

Uses and benefits

Used fresh or dried as a spice in southern Thai curries and as a natural yellow dye.

Precautions

Cooking amounts are normal in food. Concentrated supplements can interact with some medicines, so ask a doctor or pharmacist first.
