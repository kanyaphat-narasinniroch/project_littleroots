อัญชัน – Butterfly pea

Herbal Plants
Thai name
อัญชัน
Scientific name
Clitoria ternatea
Plant family
Fabaceae
Botanical features
A climbing vine with small compound leaves and deep blue-violet flowers shaped like a pea blossom.
Origin and habitat
Found in tropical Asia; often grows on fences and trellises in Thai gardens.

Uses and benefits

The flowers give a natural blue colour to drinks and sweets. Adding lime juice changes the colour toward purple-pink.

Precautions

Pregnant people and anyone taking medicine should check with a doctor before drinking it often.
