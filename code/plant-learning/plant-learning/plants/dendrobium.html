กล้วยไม้สกุลหวาย – Dendrobium orchid

Ornamental Plants
Thai name
กล้วยไม้สกุลหวาย
Scientific name
Dendrobium spp.
Plant family
Orchidaceae
Botanical features
Cane-like stems carry long sprays of flowers in white, pink, purple or yellow.
Origin and habitat
Many Dendrobium species grow wild on trees in Asian forests; farms grow hybrids.

Uses and benefits

Grown commercially as cut flowers and potted plants for decoration and gifts.

Precautions

Orchids need good airflow and should not sit in water. Wild orchids should not be collected.
