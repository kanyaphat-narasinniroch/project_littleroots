พริกขี้หนู – Bird's eye chili

Edible Plants
Thai name
พริกขี้หนู
Scientific name
Capsicum frutescens
Plant family
Solanaceae
Botanical features
A small bush with narrow upright fruits that turn from green to red when ripe.
Origin and habitat
The chili family comes from tropical America and was brought to Southeast Asia, where it became a staple.

Uses and benefits

Eaten fresh, pounded in nam phrik and pastes, or added whole to soups and stir-fries.

Precautions

Very hot. Wash your hands after cutting it, keep it away from eyes, and use small amounts if you are not used to spicy food.
