เฟื่องฟ้า – Bougainvillea

Ornamental Plants
Thai name
เฟื่องฟ้า
Scientific name
Bougainvillea spp.
Plant family
Nyctaginaceae
Botanical features
A thorny climber. The bright 'petals' are really bracts surrounding tiny white flowers.
Origin and habitat
Native to South America; thrives in hot, sunny Thai gardens.

Uses and benefits

Trained over fences, arches and walls, or pruned into shrubs and pots for colour.

Precautions

Thorns can scratch, so wear gloves when pruning.
