กระจูด – Krajood (grey sedge)

Economic Crops
Thai name
กระจูด
Scientific name
Lepironia articulata
Plant family
Cyperaceae
Botanical features
A tall sedge with round, hollow, grey-green stems that grow in dense clumps.
Origin and habitat
Grows in freshwater swamps and peat wetlands, including areas of southern Thailand.

Uses and benefits

Stems are dried, flattened and woven into mats, baskets and bags by community craft groups.

Precautions

Wild stands depend on healthy wetlands, so harvest and craft work should be done sustainably.
